# Strengths and How I Work

## My Superpower
My superpower is logical problem-solving with the ability to look at a problem from different angles. I don’t jump to a technical fix. I first ask why the problem exists and what the real goal is. The slow dashboard is my example: it looked like a dashboard problem, but the cause was in the queries, joins and the amount of data behind it, so I fixed the data logic instead of just making the screen look faster. Sometimes the problem you see on the screen isn’t the real problem, so I go one level deeper.

## What People Come to Me For
SQL, data issues, Python logic and analytical problems, and turning a requirement spoken in business language into clear technical steps. I’m comfortable asking questions of senior stakeholders when something is unclear, and I believe a simpler approach can beat a complicated one.

## A Habit I'm Proud Of
I’m never satisfied with something that just works. I ask why it works and whether it can be better. A query that gives the right answer is one thing, a query that does it efficiently on millions of rows is another. For AI applications, connecting a model to data isn’t enough; I think about retrieval quality, speed, accuracy and scale. I keep learning by experimenting and reading documentation.

## Something People Don't Expect from a Data Scientist
I’m comfortable with the business and people side. I enjoy understanding requirements, talking to stakeholders and linking a technical solution to business value, and my IIM Amritsar studies helped. I can switch between talking to a developer about a technical problem and explaining the business meaning to a non-technical person. I want to understand the problem well enough to ask whether we’re building the right thing in the first place.
