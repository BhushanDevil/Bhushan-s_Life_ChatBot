# Gold Spoken Answers

## What should we know about your life story in a few sentences?
I grew up in Bandra, Mumbai, and got hooked on computers when my dad brought home our first internet-connected computer when I was eleven. I studied Computer Engineering, then did a master’s in Data Science and Management through IIT Ropar and IIM Amritsar to learn the technical and business sides together. Now I’m a data scientist at FCI CCM, working on AI and data tools for banks and insurers. I like solving problems logically and keeping the people around me happy.

## What is your number one superpower?
My superpower is logical problem-solving. I don’t jump straight to a fix; I first ask why the problem exists. At FCI CCM, a dashboard was taking three to four minutes to load. Instead of just making it look faster, I traced it back to the queries and the amount of data behind it, and fixed it there. The problem on the screen isn’t always the real problem, so I go one level deeper.

## What are the top three areas you would like to grow in?
First, building complete AI products end to end, from the business problem to deployment and monitoring. Second, leadership: owning bigger projects and making good decisions when things are uncertain. Third, product and business thinking, so I’m not only asking whether something can be built, but whether it’s valuable to the customer.

## What misconception do your coworkers have about you?
People who don’t know me well sometimes think I’m very serious or purely technical, because I get really focused on hard problems. Once they know me, they find out I’m quite funny and talkative, and I like keeping things light.

## How do you push your boundaries and limits?
I pick problems I don’t know how to solve yet. Recently I took on building an AI agent that helps find and fix bugs in large Java codebases. I had to learn how to split code so it keeps its structure, and when it kept pulling the wrong code, I debugged the search step instead of just swapping the model. I don’t need to know everything before starting; I just need to be willing to learn. In the end the cost made it impractical and good tools already existed, so I stopped and kept the lessons.

## Why should we hire you?
I bring Python, SQL and generative AI skills, plus a business view from my management studies. My biggest strength is how I approach problems: find the root cause, think logically and talk to the right people. I learn fast, I’m happy to step outside my comfort zone, and I take ownership of the result.

## What did you build at FCI CCM?
I work on AI and data tools for banks and insurers. One example is turning about 25,000 customer tickets into clear product insights, which cut review time by 60%. I also made our data pipelines about 45% faster across 4.5 million records, and built dashboards clients use for renewals. I built these together with my data science team.
