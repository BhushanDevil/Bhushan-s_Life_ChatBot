# Education

## Rizvi College of Engineering, Mumbai
B.E. in Computer Engineering (2018 to 2022). I chose it because I was curious how software and systems work behind the apps we use every day, and I liked turning ideas into something practical through code. What I enjoyed most was building a problem-solving mindset: programming, databases, software development and analytical thinking. It taught me that technology is about understanding a problem and finding an effective solution, not just writing code.

## IIT Ropar and IIM Amritsar
M.S. in Data Science and Management (2023 to 2025). As I went further, I became interested in how data can solve larger business problems. I liked that this programme combined two views: IIT Ropar for the technical and analytical side, and IIM Amritsar for the business and management side. I didn’t want to be someone who could only build models. I wanted to understand how data-driven solutions help organisations make better decisions.

The hardest part was the mix of subjects. Coming from engineering, I was comfortable with programming, but I had to strengthen statistics, machine learning and analytical methods, while management subjects pushed me to think about business goals and stakeholders. I handled it by staying consistent, learning step by step, working on practical problems and connecting classes with real situations.

How I see it: my education was a progression. Computer Engineering taught me to build solutions, Data Science taught me to get insights from information, and Management taught me to think about the value those solutions create.
