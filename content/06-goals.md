# Goals and Why Me

## Three to Five Year Goal
In three to five years I see myself as a strong AI professional who can independently design, build and deliver end-to-end AI solutions, going deeper into generative AI, agentic AI, machine learning, RAG and scalable AI applications. I want to own larger projects, work closely with stakeholders, and eventually lead a team or initiative where I influence both technical decisions and business strategy. The real achievement is when people use a solution and it creates measurable value.

## Why AI and Data Science
It brings together technology, logical problem-solving and practical solutions. At FCI CCM I saw how AI can help real business problems. I’m especially excited about generative and agentic AI, where systems can find information, reason, use tools and support complex workflows, and I enjoy making them useful, reliable and scalable.

## Why Hire Me
I bring technical skills in Python, SQL, machine learning, analytics and generative AI, together with a business view from my management studies. My biggest strength is how I approach problems: find the root cause, think logically, talk to the right people, and find practical solutions. I learn fast, I’m comfortable stepping outside my comfort zone, and I will take ownership. I may not know every tool on day one, but I’m confident I can learn, adapt and contribute.

## How I Think Under Pressure
Life isn’t always fair and not everything is in my control, so I put my energy into what I can change. I’m an emotional person too, but I try not to let emotions make decisions for me. Whether it’s a work problem, a family issue or a money question, I first understand the problem, separate facts from assumptions, and then look at solutions. That keeps me fairly calm under pressure. I don’t expect things to go to plan, but I believe there is always something constructive to do.
